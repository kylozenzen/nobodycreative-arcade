NOBODY ARCADE PHASE 1.5 — NETLIFY

This package is already flattened correctly.
The ZIP root contains index.html.

MANUAL DEPLOY
1. Open your site in Netlify.
2. Go to Deploys.
3. Upload nobody-arcade-phase1-5-netlify-ready.zip.
4. Wait for Published.
5. Hard-refresh the live site once so the new service worker takes control.

IMPORTANT
- Do not unzip it and then upload the parent folder.
- PWA installation requires the HTTPS Netlify URL.
- Update data/games.js with your deployed game URLs.
- Replace hello@example.com in index.html.
